question: "What is APSEDEC?"
answer: "APSEDEC is the Acholi Private Sector Development Company Limited, a national capacity-building and business-development organisation in Uganda. It was established in June 1999 as a project under the Government of Uganda and UNDP Country Cooperation Framework, and registered in June 2004 as a Company Limited by Guarantee (Registration No. 62106). It was formerly the Kitgum Private Sector Promotion Centre."
