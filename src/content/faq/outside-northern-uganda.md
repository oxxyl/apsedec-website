question: "Does APSEDEC only work in Northern Uganda?"
answer: "No. APSEDEC is a national organisation. Alongside its Acholi and Lango work it delivers in Busoga through the National Oilseeds Project, in Buvuma, Mayuge, Namayingo and Bugiri through NOPP-FID, in Kalangala, and across 15 districts of Central and Western Uganda through the Agriculture Value Chain Development Programme."
