Establishment and strengthening of Community Savings and Credit Groups (CSCGs/VSLAs) to expand rural financial inclusion — Phase 2.1 Part 1 (Apr 2016 – Apr 2019), Phase 2.1 Part 2, and the mature-group "Strengthening, Innovations & Partnerships" Phase 2.2 (Dec 2018 – Dec 2021), with financial literacy, business skills and bank linkages. Delivered in consortium with MNPSDC and IIRR.

## Scale and reach

3,486 CSCGs with 59,726 members trained across Phases 1 and 2, plus 211 mature CSCGs (5,680 members) strengthened.
