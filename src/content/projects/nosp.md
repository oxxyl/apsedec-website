Delivered as the **SEPSPEL–APSEDEC Joint Venture**, this Agribusiness
Development Service Provision assignment commercialises the oilseed value chain
through farmer institutional development, agricultural extension, financial
inclusion, market-systems strengthening and inclusive agribusiness across the
eleven Busoga districts.

## Scale and reach

630 farmer groups and 18,175 farming households targeted over the three-year
period.
