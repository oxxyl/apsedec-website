Business development services promoting self-employment, small-scale entrepreneurship, vocational skilling and rural finance for oil-palm and non-oil-palm households, helping youth and women diversify their livelihoods through profitable enterprises and savings groups.

## Scale and reach

1,150 beneficiaries (50% women, 50% youth) trained in entrepreneurship and vocational skills, with operational business plans, profitable enterprises and 56 VSLAs formed.
