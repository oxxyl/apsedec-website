Technical capacity-building of Mount Otce Metu Savings and Credit Society (Metu
Trading Centre, Metu Subcounty) to meet MSE Recovery Fund eligibility —
strengthening corporate governance, financial performance, credit and risk
management, and information systems using the STAR approach. Funded under FSD
Uganda's Affirmative Action MSE Recovery Fund.

## Scale and reach

Metu SACCO serves 7,908 members and 1,516 active loan clients on a loan book of
UGX 2.3 billion.
