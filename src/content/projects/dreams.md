Mentoring women entrepreneurs to transition from informal to micro and small enterprises through enterprise-specific skills, financial management, record-keeping, business development, digital literacy, market linkages, value addition and gender-norms household dialogues.

## Scale and reach

3,537 women entrepreneurs targeted across Gulu City, Gulu, Kitgum, Nwoya and Omoro; 3,197 (90%) mentored and trained, with 8 of 19 result areas exceeding target.
