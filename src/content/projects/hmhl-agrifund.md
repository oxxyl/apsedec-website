APSEDEC delivers the Northern Uganda implementation node of CARE
International's multi-country **Her Money, Her Life Agrifund**, run with
Bloomberg Philanthropies and the Women's Economic Development Network. The
programme moves low-income women from informal activity into market-oriented
collective enterprises through savings-led inclusion, enterprise development
and gender-norms change, delivered across seven milestones.

Value chains: oilseeds, maize, coffee and hibiscus.

## Scale and reach

APSEDEC component — 150 VSLAs strengthened, 150 collective marketing
committees, 25 PMGs/aggregation hubs formalised and 50 women leaders mentored.
Contract ceiling UGX 444M. The wider programme targets 30,000 women directly.
