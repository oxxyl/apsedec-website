Delivered as the APSEDEC Consortium with the International Institute of Rural Reconstruction (IIRR). Technical agricultural extension and agribusiness development for the maize and rice value chains — farmer institutional development, farming-as-a-business, post-harvest handling, local seed business, the Village Agent Model and VSLAs.

## Scale and reach

14,591 farmer groups (323,868 members) reached and 1,462 Village Agents established; 91% of cumulative deliverables achieved.
