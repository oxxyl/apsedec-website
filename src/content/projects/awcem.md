A research-driven CARE International programme designing, testing and scaling
inclusive market-aggregation and credit models for low-income, women-led
enterprises, building on prior DREAMS and DDT4C programming.

Value chains: groundnuts, hibiscus, seed oils and shea butter.

## Scale and reach

APSEDEC component — 331 women's groups (6,620 women entrepreneurs) and 50 women
business mentors across 8 Northern Uganda districts. The wider programme targets
12,000 women across 600 VSLAs in Uganda.
