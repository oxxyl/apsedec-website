Support for out-of-school adolescent youth (15–17 years) engaged in or at risk of hazardous labour, enabling decent work and self-employment.

## Scale and reach

More than 900 youth and their household members supported, with 255 trained in alternative enterprises and 780 households establishing backyard gardens.
