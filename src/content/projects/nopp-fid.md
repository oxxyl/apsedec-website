Under the National Oil Palm Project, APSEDEC forms and strengthens Oil Palm
Growers' Organisations (OPGOs) and their finance arms (SACCOs) for sustainable
service delivery — a three-year formation phase followed by two years of
monitoring and sustainability. Hubs cover Buvuma plus Mayuge, Namayingo and
Bugiri districts.

## Scale and reach

Four cooperatives formed and registered (Buvuma OPGO and the Mayuge, Namayingo
and Bugiri District OPGOs); 2,750 oil-palm grower members mobilised across the
two hubs, with SACCO development underway.
